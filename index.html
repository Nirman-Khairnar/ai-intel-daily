EU AI Act Omnibus: Parliament Votes to Delay High-Risk Deadlines

On June 16, the European Parliament voted 423-57 on Digital Omnibus amendments pushing high-risk AI compliance deadlines from August 2026 to December 2027 — and August 2028 for Annex I regulated products. Also: outright ban on AI generating nonconsensual intimate imagery, clarified rules for AI in industrial machinery, and carve-out for processing special category data for bias detection. The EU Council must sign off before August 2, but passage is expected. For Indian companies selling into EU: you now have 18 more months, but logging must be tamper-evident and reconstructable. Logging inputs/outputs alone is not enough.

⚠️ Watch Out

RETROACTIVE EU AI Act Is Retroactive — Existing High-Risk AI Is In Scope

The June 16 amendments did NOT make the Act purely prospective. AI systems already deployed in high-risk settings (hiring, healthcare, law enforcement, logistics routing) are still in scope. You get more TIME but not amnesty. The grace period runs from when a system was first placed on the market, not from the amendment date. Action: audit every AI touchpoint touching EU customers NOW. Log everything. The "more time" is to fix, not to ignore.
