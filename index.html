⚠️ Watch Out

The Great American AI Act — 269 Pages, Bipartisan, Controversial

risk

Reps. Obernolte (R-CA) and Trahan (D-MA) released a draft of the first comprehensive federal AI governance regime. It would preempt state AI laws for 3 years, organize governance into Frontier AI, Workforce, Cybersecurity, and R&D titles. Civil society groups (Public Citizen, AFL-CIO, Public Knowledge) immediately opposed the preemption. This is going to be a fight — and the outcome determines who regulates your AI stack.
