EU AI Act High-Risk Enforcement Date Approaching: August 2, 2026

The EU AI Act's high-risk AI obligations become legally enforceable on August 2, 2026 (24 months after entry into force). Despite a European Commission proposal to delay to late 2027, this extension has not been enacted into law. Fines reach up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices, and €15 million or 3% for high-risk violations. Operators using AI in logistics, routing, or customer service must begin conformity assessments now.
