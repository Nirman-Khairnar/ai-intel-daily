EU AI Act High-Risk Enforcement: August 2, 2026

The EU AI Act's high-risk AI obligations become legally enforceable on August 2, 2026 (24 months after entry into force). Triggers application of most provisions not already in force, including comprehensive requirements for high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III. Fines up to 35M EUR or 7% global turnover for prohibited practices.

Cloudflare AI Crawler Policy: Sept 15 Deadline

Cloudflare's new policy (effective July 1, 2026) gives AI companies until September 15, 2026 to separate web crawlers used for search from those used for AI training and agents. After this date, mixed-use crawlers face default blocking on many publisher sites, potentially impacting AI training data collection and agent functionality.
