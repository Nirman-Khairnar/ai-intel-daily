RISK AI Data Retention — Prompts and Outputs Stored Indefinitely

Enterprise security researchers flag that AI tools store prompts and outputs indefinitely — creating an enormous attack surface. When you feed customer data, financial records, or logistics manifests into an AI model, that data persists in training sets, logs, and caches. The business question isn't "can we use AI?" but "what data can we safely feed it?"
